# Biography

## Personal

I grew up in a very small town called Oostburg, which is located about an hour north of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. I went to college at Marquette University, getting a bachelor's and a master's degree in electrical engineering. I've been a software developer in varying roles ever since I finished college.

I've been married to Elizabeth since September 1997. In November 2000, we made the decision to move to Minnesota, and we eventually bought a house in Shakopee, which is where we've been since 2001. We have two kids, Hayden and Felix.

I have a number of hobbies:
* Music - I've played an instrument since I was 11 (more or less). My mainstay is guitar, but I also play bass guitar, drums, and keyboards.
* Reading - I've always loved just sitting around and reading a book. I have **way** too many books to read these days - my eventual goal is to donate them to libraries.
* Coding - It's my day job, but it's also something I like to do off-hours. It's a form of puzzle solving, and it's a way to keep my brain active.
* Streaming - I have a YouTube channel (link is below), and I've created a bunch of coding videos over the years. Recently I've started to stream some coding sessions and video games that I enjoy. Feel free to subscribe!
* Self-defense - I started doing kempo karate in 2020 with my older kid, who had been doing it longer than me and was always bugging me to give it a try. I'm currently a 2nd degree brown belt. The dojo I go to is [Zen Dragon Martial Arts](https://zendragonmartialarts.com/).

## Professional

Jason Bock is a Staff Software Engineer at Rocket Mortgage and a Microsoft MVP (C#). He has nearly 30 years of experience working on several business applications using a diverse set of frameworks and languages. He is the author of a number of books, such as ".NET Development Using the Compiler API", "Metaprogramming in .NET", and "Applied .NET Attributes". He has written numerous articles on software development issues and has presented at conferences and user groups over his career.
