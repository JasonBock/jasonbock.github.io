# The Universe is Open-Source

Brent "Pupp-Grinder" Rasmussen has an [excellent post](https://web.archive.org/web/20090106053932/http://brentrasmussen.com/log/node/717) on atheism. I particulary like this quote:

> Nothing is hidden. Nothing is secret. There are no tricks built into the universe, no lies, no tests of loyalty, no conspiracy to keep us small and humble and ignorant. Nothing is willfully denied us. It’s all right out where we can see it. And if we’re smart enough, to figure it out.

While I like the phrase "never say never", there's no way I could ever join a religious institution. Brent said so clearly: just look up to the sky, or look around you - that's reality. To me, that's humbling and awe-inspiring.
