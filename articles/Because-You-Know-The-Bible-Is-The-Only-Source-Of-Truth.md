# Because, You Know, The Bible is the Only Source of Truth

Now, personally, I think anyone should have the right to wear what they want to wear. I wouldn't wear a t-shirt that had the ten commandments on it, but go for it if that's your thang. And, really, if a judge wants to wear a cross or a pentagram or whatever, I don't care, so long as he/she makes decisions based on the law. However, what really concerns me is that a judge is saying that "you can't divorce the law from the truth...The Ten Commandments can help a judge know the difference between right and wrong", I get worried, very worried. Since when does one set of religious laws trump another?
