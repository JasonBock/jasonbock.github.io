# 10 Years Ago Today ...

I've been digging around in Xamlon, getting familiar with the engine (I'll post my thoughts about the tool later on, probably after Code Camp II), but I stumbled across a blog that had the "what was I doing 10 years ago today?" entry. Which made me think ... what was I doing 10 years ago? Hmmm ... let's see. I was in my 2nd year of graduate school, having a grand old time, coding up my number theoretic transform, living on Marquette's campus, and basically really enjoying my life. Funny how one month later it would be completely turned upside down when my fiance unexpectedly broke things off for someone else :). Man, was that **10** years ago? Where has my life gone?! :)
