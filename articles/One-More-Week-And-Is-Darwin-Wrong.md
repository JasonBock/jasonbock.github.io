# One More Week, and is Darwin Wrong?

One more week and I'm back at a client site doing development. You don't know how much I'm looking forward to getting back on the horse and get away from the technical writing stuff.

My wife grabbed the Nov. 2004 issue of National Geographic from work. The title article is called, "Was Darwin Wrong?", which is prominently featured on the cover. What I loved is how the article starts. In huge letters, there's one word: "NO." Bravo! The article does a great job summing up the evolutionary theory and basically obliterates any notion that creationism has any merit in the scientific classroom. Unfortunately, the creationists will continually wage war in the political arenas to stop science from being taught, so articles like this are sorely needed.
