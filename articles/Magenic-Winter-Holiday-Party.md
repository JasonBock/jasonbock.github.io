# Magenic Winter Holiday Party

Last night Magenic had their annual winter holiday festivites at the Mill Road Museum. I had a good time. Free drinks, free food, and gambling! Well, the gambling was to win tickets to put in bags that were marked for prizes. I only won 4 tickets, so I put 'em all in one bag, and I won the Best Buy gift certificate. Having a party like this reminds me how nice it is to work for Magenic. No company is perfect and Magenic has its quirks, but they treat their employees **very** well, and it's just a good company to work for.
