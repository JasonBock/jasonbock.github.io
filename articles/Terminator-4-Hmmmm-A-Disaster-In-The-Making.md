# Terminator 4 ... Hmmmm ... A Disaster in the Making?

Personally, I liked "Terminator 3". But, admittedly, it was my least favorite of the three (although I loved the chase scene in the beginning - just amazing). Now, though, rumors are circulating that there will be a fourth "Terminator" movie. Since Arnold has some political duties to attend to and Nick and Claire haven't signed on just yet, I think this series is pretty much done. Of course, if they got The Rock to play a new Terminator it may pull people in, but ... I don't know. This series should've ended with a 3rd movie that was completely based in the future war scenario, but the 3rd movie just didn't work with the other two.
