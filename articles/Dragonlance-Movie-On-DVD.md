# Dragonlance Movie on DVD

I remember reading the Dragonlance books when I was in high school. Sure, they're not examples of literary greatness, but I enjoyed them. I especially like the character of Raistlin. Now there's an animated movie that's gone straight to DVD with Lucy Lawless and Keifer Sutherland doing voice work. Kiefer does Raistlan...which may be really cool or cheesy. The whole idea seems cheesy...I may need to buy it!
