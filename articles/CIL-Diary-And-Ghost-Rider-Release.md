# CIL Diary, And "Ghost Rider" Release

I finally got the CIL diary in PDF format. Click [here](./Diary-CIL.pdf) to download the ZIP file.

Speaking of diaries, Neil Peart (drummer of Rush) should have his book Ghost Rider out any day now. I didn't really know what the book was about until I read the book description. I thought it was going to be a collection of reflections (hmmm ... that sounds like a cool .NET book title, "Collections of Reflections") with his life in Rush, but it's about his struggle with losing his daughter and wife (both within one year). This will be my next book to read when it's published - no doubt about it. I really liked The Masked Rider, and I bet the new book will be just as good, if not better.
