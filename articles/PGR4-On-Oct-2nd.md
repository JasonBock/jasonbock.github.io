# PGR4 On Oct. 2nd

Honestly, I don't know what to make of the next version. PGR3 was a letdown in a lot of ways, and now PGR4 is adding motorcycles. That feels more gimmicky to me than anything. Maybe it'll be really cool, but ... what's next, go-karts in PGR5? PGR2 was so much fun (I still see people playing that over PGR3 on my friends list) - I hope PGR4 brings that back.
