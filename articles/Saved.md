# "Saved!"

Just saw this movie. I absolutely **loved** it. There was so much in the movie that made me laugh. I used to run in the crowd that was depicted in the movie, and it was funny to see it from another angle. One of the best lines was when Mandy Moore's characters throws a bible at the main character, Jena Malone (I believe is the name), and says, "I am filled with Christ's love!" To which she retorts, "This is not a weapon!" Beautiful, just beautiful.
