# No More Notebooks

A while ago I blogged about the notebooks I had created over the years. What's interesting is that, in the last year I've gone notebook-less. I hardly ever write in it anymore. Usually I just bust open Word or notepad++ and use a file to track my train of thought. While I'm a lover of books, I am trying to get away from using paper at the office (I know, there's a big irony there). Occasionally I'll scratch some thoughts together on paper but it's becoming a rarity.

I wonder when kids won't be taught to write anymore; everything will be done on a computer via some interface (keyboard, mouse, neural I/O, etc.).
