# Easter Madness

We had about 20 relatives in our house this past weekend. If you've been to my house you know that's a lot of people to fit into our casa. Plus, they're mostly Liz's family, and while I get along with them fine, they tend to be a lot more talkative than what I'm used to, so after a while I feel a bit overwhelmed. Hayden did OK with the sudden influx of people, although he didn't like it Sunday when everybody was getting into Marble Blast on the 360 and yelling/screaming whenever someone would go over the edge - that upset him a bit. Overall I had fun, but when everyone left Sunday Liz and I just sat on the couch and relaxed, enjoying the quiet :).

My next task is to get my web site/blog/UI tool updated this week. Then I'm getting the desk designed/estimated/built.

I'm also grooving on my new JVC camcorder (although by stating that fact that's now a guarantee that it'll break ;) ). It has a 30 GB hard drive in it so I don't have to worry about tapes, discs, etc.
