# The Joys of Working at Home, and Marquette ... What the Hell Happened?

It's so nice to be able to wake up, have a 10-second "commute" to the office, and have a cat hop up onto your lap, purr, sigh, and fall asleep. What could be better?

Saturday night - that was truly painful. It's not that Marquette wasn't getting good looks at the basket, but they couldn't hit a damn thing. I give credit to Kansas - they're definitely a good team. But Marquette truly took themselves out in humiliating fashion.
