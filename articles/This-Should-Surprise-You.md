# This Should Surprise You

So I download a ton of music last night, and then I sat there this morning, thinking, "which album should I play first?"

You'll never guess which one I picked.

If you thought Rich Mullins' "The World As Best As I Remember It, Vol. 1", then you're correct.

Yeah, it's an overtly-religious CD. But for some reason I always liked Rich's music on this CD, and his voice is really good. I figured, hell, download it and see if it's as good as **I** remember it :). I saw him play in concert twice - seeing him play the dulcimer on "Calling Out Your Name" was really cool.

This reminds me, I need to find some Phil Keaggy ...
