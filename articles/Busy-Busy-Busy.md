# Busy, Busy, Busy

I usually don't take such a long break from blogging, but right now I've got a lot going on. My main focus these days (other than family and the day job) is my book. Yes, I'm writing another book. It's called "Metaprogramming in .NET", and I'm doing it with Kevin Hazzard. It's a lot of work, and trying to write a book with 2 kids is an adventure in and of itself, but I'm truly loving it. I'm also planning for the next Twin Cities Code Camp - this next event (April 9th and 10th) will be number 10, and I'm really looking foward to it. I also have a bunch of speaking engagements planned in the next couple of months - this includes the Twin Cities, Chippewa Valley, Mankato, Appleton, and (probably) Madison.

I'm not going anywhere - my blogging frequency will just be low for a while. I should be done with the book by summertime so I plan to do more writing then. Oh, I am pretty active on Twitter - feel free to follow my random thoughts at @jasonbock.
