# Starting a New Client This Week

I'm going out to a new client this week, probably on Wednesday. It's all ASP .NET using C# with a possibility of going to 2.0 in the very near future. The work seems interesting and challenging, which is always a good thing in my book, but with any new client, I have no idea how it's going to turn out [1]. I've learned to not have any expectations, good or bad or anywhere in between. I just go in with the best attitude I can and let it go from there.

[1] I mean, if I could predict the future, I'd be swimming in a pool of Benjamins right now.
