# Golfing Dichotomy

This morning I played a round at Deer Run Golf Course. The first nine did not go well - I shot a 50. But, something clicked on the back nine, and I shot a 39 to finish with a 89. That was a great way to finish. I have no idea what started to work so well on the back nine, but it was a nice way to close out my golfing run during my vacation.
