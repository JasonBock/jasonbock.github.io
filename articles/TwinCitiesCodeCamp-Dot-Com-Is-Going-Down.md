# twincitiescodecamp.com is Going Down

In a short amount of time, [this site](https://twincitiescodecamp.com/) will go down:

![TCCC Web Site](https://jasonbock.net/images/TCCC.png "TCCC Web Site")

Three years ago, we had our final event, TCCC24. Covid shut us down for 4 years, and while our last event went as well as I thought it would after such a lengthy hiatus, I felt like the time had come to shut the doors. My fellow members on the leadership team agreed. We updated the site to let folks know of our decision, and we decided to let the site expire on its own time.

That time is rapidly approaching. Soon, it will exist no more.

I'm incredibly proud to have been a part of TCCC since Day 1. I have a ton of memories from all 24 events. A lot of hard work went in to organizing, planning, and delivering each event. And without a doubt, we created [the best damn code camp theme song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L1Q1gw8fhUE) in history.

17 years. 24 events. Hundreds of speakers. Thousands of attendees. Always free. And a helluva fun time.
