# Loving "Hope"

"Snakes and Arrows" is definitely growing on me - it may just be my favorite Rush CD of all time. For some reason I love the instrumental "Hope". It's a simple 2-minute Alex-on-the-acoustic piece, but the title captures the mood of the song perfectly. I really hope he does in concert and expands on it just a bit.
