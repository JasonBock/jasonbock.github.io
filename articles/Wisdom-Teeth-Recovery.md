# Wisdom Teeth Recovery

Yesterday I got my wisdom teeth pulled. It was the first time I ever went under, and it was kind of weird. The doctor put the drugs in my IV, I started to feel a little dizzy, then they asked me to put this block-like device in my mouth ... and that's all I remember until they asked me to wake up.

Overall things have been going OK so far. I've been able to manage the pain today with nothing more than Advil every 4 hours. The bleeding seems to be tapering off. That doesn't mean that everything is OK yet, but I'm trying to lay low and just relax in hopes that things continue on the same path.
