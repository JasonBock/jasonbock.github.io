# Web Page Fun

A client I was at for a year until a couple of months ago is getting ready to release the last web project I worked on while I was there. If you go here, click on the link top-center that's subtitled, "Get a sneak preview of MarketWatch's new and improved front page." It's not officially launched, but at least you can see where they're going with their sites. There's all sorts of cool asynchronous server- and client-side processes going on to create that page, and I'm pretty happy with it. Even though I'm not there to see it through to completion, I know what it took to get that working, and it was a lot of fun.
