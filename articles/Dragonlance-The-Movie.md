# Dragonlance ... The Movie?!

I just found out about it. I remember reading the first two trilogies when I was in high school and I really liked them (after that the series devolved into a bunch of authors writing about pretty much anything they wanted, although Hickman and Weis have also made contributions - those are the ones I'll read). I'm not sure if this will work, although having it animated is probably a better choice, and for some reason I can see Keifer Sutherland's voice as Raistlin.
