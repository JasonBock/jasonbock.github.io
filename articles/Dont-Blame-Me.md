# Don't Blame Me ...

OK, it's not official yet. But it looks like Bush is going to get a 2nd term. I'm not so much a Kerry supporter as one who just does not like Bush, but now that I voted against Bush it's going to sicken me to hear Bush talk about what "the American people" voted for. Sigh ... at least I voted this time so I can bitch, moan, whine, and complain as Bush takes away more of our rights and increases our war machine.
