# The Dover Case

The intelligent design trial in Dover began this week. It's getting lots of attention from numerous media/news sources. Last night when I was working out I saw a discussion between Eugenie Scott (I believe that's her name) and somebody from a creationist movement (for lack of a better work). Since I could only read the closed-captioning and I was sweating up a storm I didn't catch the entire segment but I caught most of it and Scott buried the creationist. She really had him on the ropes but in a subtle way, trapping him in his own creationist "logic." I can only hope that the court rules for the parents that sued the school in this case so ID can be exposed to the world for what it really is: just another creationist group trying to push their creator through the public schools.
