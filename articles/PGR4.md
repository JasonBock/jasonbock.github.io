# PGR4

I completely forgot that PGR4 came out this week - d'oh! I haven't had time to play it yet, but I'm going to try and give it a whirl this weekend. PGR was great, PGR2 was excellent (and its Live experience has been the best I've had in any game so far), but PGR3 was a letdown. Now they added motorcycles ... I'm just not sure about that, but I won't know until I try it.
