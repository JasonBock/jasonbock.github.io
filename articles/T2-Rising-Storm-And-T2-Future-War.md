# "T2: Rising Storm" and "T2: Future War"

After reading the first book ("T2: Infiltrator"), I decided to give the other 2 books in the series a whirl. The first book wasn't stellar, but it was interesting. Unfortunately, I can't say the same thing for the other two in the trilogy. It seems like Stirling was trying to get done with these books, so the story feels like it's speeding up in terms of pace. By the time you get near the end of "Future War", it really feels rushed. It's too bad, because the first book had some promise.
