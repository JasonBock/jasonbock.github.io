# Renaming the Little Guy

After a week of calling our new kitty Butters, we've decided to rename him to Tweak. We did the same thing with Tut - we called him Linus as first, but it was too close to Simon to make it confusing. Tut and Butters was too similiar as well, but we wanted to keep the South Park theme with the kitty, so we went with Tweak as he's a real spastic kitten.

Note: As of yesterday, we're 14 weeks pregnant. So, hopefully we'll have more pressing things to worry about come mid-March than what our cat names are :).
