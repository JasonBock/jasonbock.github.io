# Anniversary Celebration

This past Tuesday Liz and I had our 8th anniversary. We celebrated it last night. We went out to Dangerfield's in Shakopee. Overall the food was good but the atmosphere had kind of a cheesy late 70s feel to it (and what was up with that cheesy player piano music?). Then we went to a bookstore to kill some time before we went to see "Flightplan". Overall I liked the movie, although Jodie Foster's work carries a lot of the story. The plot line gets stretched pretty thin at the end, but that's OK - movies don't always have to be realistic, and I enjoyed the movie for what it was: entertainment.
