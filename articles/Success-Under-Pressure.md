# "Success Under Pressure"

If you're a die-hard Rush fan, you remember that there was book published in the mid-80s about them called, "Success Under Pressure." I used to have it, but unfortunately I lost it. These days, finding a copy can cost you a pretty penny from someone who's willing to sell their used copy, but if you want to peruse the material, someone has posted the contents. I doubt that this is a legal thing to do, but it's fun to go over its' material.
