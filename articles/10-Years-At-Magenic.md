# 10 Years at Magenic

Today I got an e-mail from my employer, Magenic, that I've been with the company for 10 years.

Ten. Years.

**Wow**.

I honestly never expected to ever be with a company for this long! Before I joined Magenic, I hopped between companies, never staying anywhere for longer than 2 years. Over the years, Magenic has grown on me, and it's been a great place for me to be challenged by a lot of smart folks, grow in my career, and have some tremendous experiences as a software developer.

Is it "the" place to be? No. But that's because I don't think there's **any** "the" place to be. Everyone has to find their niche to be successful, and Magenic is that place for me. Maybe Magenic is that place for you as well. If you're looking for a place to stretch yourself as a software developer and as a consultant, contact them and maybe I'll see you on a gig of ours in the future :).
