# My Yearly Review

I just got done with my yearly review with Magenic. Overall I'm happy with the results. I'm still not making a 7-figure salary, but that'll come with time ;). The thing I've been struggling with is, "where do I go from here?" It's not that I feel like I know everything there is to know about technology and software development, but I don't really know what direction I want my career to go in. I think there were some good ideas that came up in the meeting, but I haven't had the "aha!" moment just yet. Believe me, I'm happy with my job and where things are going with Magenic; I just need to figure out what I need to do now to try and keep things interesting for years to come.
