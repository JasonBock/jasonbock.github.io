# Local Fireworks Display and Weekend Trip

Last night my neighbors did their annual local fireworks display, which pretty much amounts to shooting off things that may or may not be of a legal nature. I've learned that fireworks create more energy in children than sugar ever could.

I'm also heading down to WI this weekend to stop by my mom's house to pick up some of my dad's tools. She's wanted me to do this for a while now, but I've never been able to find a weekend where I could do it. Plus, I need to make the trip myself; if Liz and Hayden are along, the car is too full to bring any tools back. The deck is looming in my mind, though, and I need to make sure I have the tools to do the job, so I'm making the solo trek down to see what I can find.
