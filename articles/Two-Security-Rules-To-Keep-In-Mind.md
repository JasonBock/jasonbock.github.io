# Two Security Rules to Keep In Mind

There are lots of things that we all need to keep in mind from a security perspective when we're dealing with computers. Here are two at the current client that we're finding are essential to our personal well-being:

* Don't walk away from your computer with it unlocked.
* Change your default password when you get a new computer.

Lots of prankster-ish things await you if you forget to follow these rules ...
