# "Portal" From "The Orange Box"

After a while of not playing any games on my 360, I put "The Orange Box" in and played "Portal".

Wow. What a fun, **fun** game! It's based on a very simple concept, but it's done so well, and the running commentary is very funny. Highly recommended.
