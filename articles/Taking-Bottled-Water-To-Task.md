# Taking Bottled Water to Task

I always thought there was something fishy about bottled water - this article [1] has an interesting take on the whole matter. I know people who will simply not drink tap water because there are "bad" things in it, and they won't bat an eye when they spend 2, 3, even 4 dollars on a bottle of water.

No wonder "Evian" is naive spelled backwards.
