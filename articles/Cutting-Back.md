# Cutting Back

I've never been good at doing goals/resolutions at the end of the year. That said, I've decided to cut back on two things, and it just coincides with the beginning of this year. One, I'm getting rid of soda (so is Liz). Two, I'm trying to limit the amount of TV I watch. It's not that I think TV is evil, but it can be a time-sucker if I'm not careful. There's only about 2 or 3 shows that I watch religiously, but other than that it's just channel surfing, and there's better ways I can spend my time.
