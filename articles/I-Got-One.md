# I Got One!

Today was the day. The product I have been waiting for. Today was the release date. I was worried that local stores may not have a unit available, but I had faith. I waited until I was done with work, and then I hit a local Best Buy. I tried not to run in and look like a total geek, but I was really stoked for this thing. And ... wow! I was amazed - they had a copy!

![Rush R30](https://jasonbock.net/images/Rush-R30.png "Rush R30")

Yes! It's Rush's 30th Anniversary World Tour DVD! Yahoo!!!

What, did you think I was referring to that "other" thing that was released today? Sheesh, give me a break!
