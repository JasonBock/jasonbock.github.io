# Bridge Collapse - We're OK

There was a bridge collapse in the Twin Cities today, just north of Minneapolis. Fortunately, I don't take that bridge when I got to work, so we're OK, but holy shit. This will definitely cause major traffic problems for the Twin Cities. And yes, there are people dead because of the collapse, very, very sad. A former co-worker at a client I was at a year ago took some amazing pictures. Wow.

There's a video of the collapse as it happened on CNN. The vid is surreal to watch.
