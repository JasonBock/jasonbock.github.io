# A Popular MP3?

I've just been informed that my site usage has been pretty high this past month. After looking at the logs, it shocked me to find out that one of my MP3 files is getting downloaded at a ridiculous rate - a total of 18 gigs. Yikes! It looks like the biggest offender is some aol.com site. Sigh ... sometimes I wonder if it's even worth publishing a site anymore. I'm getting really tired of handling some of the things I've run into lately.
