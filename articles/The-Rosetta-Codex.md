# "The Rosetta Codex"

I finished this book this morning - I read most of it on the flight to Redmond yesterday. I read "Ship of Fools" a while ago and I really enjoyed it, and this book was no exception. It's basically the story about a guy named Cole who has led a difficult life, but during his travels he finds a book from an alien civilization that may be the key to resurrecting a dead race. It's not a difficult read, but for some reason I found the storyline pretty engaging. Definitely recommended.
