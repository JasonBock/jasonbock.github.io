# Lyric Recall

I'm listening to a bunch of old CDs on my Zune, and I'm **amazed** at how easily all of the lyrics are coming back to me. For example, right now I'm listening to "Frontiers" from Journey (I loved the B side of this album when I was younger; the A side was pretty much a bunch of sappy-crappy songs) and memories of singing (and playing guitar to) "Troubled Child" and "Edge of the Blade" are flooding back. Man, to be a kid again ... :)
