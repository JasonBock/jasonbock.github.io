# YACR (Yet Another CIL Reader)

I just found out about Cosmos, an OS that's written in C# (the IL is translated to x86). It's interesting that they have their own ILReader.

Sigh ...

What kinds of makes me sad about this is that this is need that keeps popping up here and there (I mentioned this a while ago). Now there's a good open-source assembly reader/writer in Cecil but it would be so nice if we has this in the BCL. I doubt it'll ever happen ... but one can hope ...
