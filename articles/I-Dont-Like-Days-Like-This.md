# I Don't Like Days Like This

I woke up this morning feeling like crap (bad cold). Then I had a really hard time getting my car to turn over (don't know what the hell that was all about). Once it started, it ran just fine, but now I'm sitting at work wondering if I got a big brick of a car in the lot and I have no way of getting home (easily). Plus, I'm dealing with the 2.0 configuration stuff, and sometimes I feel it's far harder to manipulate than it should be. Of course, my brain doesn't feel like thinking right now either - colds seem to affect how my brain processes things. I know, whine, whine, whine, but I should just go home (if my car will allow it), crawl into bed, and moan for a while.
