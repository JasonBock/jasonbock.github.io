# Netflix On-Demand == Complete Suckage

Last night I updated my XBox with the new cutsey avatar thingee stuff. My main reason to upgrade was to get on Netflix and start watching cool movies.

Ugh.

Let me say that again: UGH.

The online selection is horrible. I don't want "Clash of the Titans", thank you very much; I want movies that just came out so I can watch them when I want to...because isn't that the whole point of "on-demand"?

I'll probably be dumping my one-day-old subscription when I get home. What a disappointment.
