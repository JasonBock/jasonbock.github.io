# Perfect Morning with Transatlantic

This morning's drive to Milwaukee was amazing. I started listening to "Bridge Across Forever" and just as the sun started to rise, I got to the title track. Then, as the sun got brighter, I hit the final song, "Stranger in Your Soul." Beautiful sunrise, awesome views with the trees changing colors - a great way to start the day!
