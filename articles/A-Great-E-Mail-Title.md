# A Great E-Mail Title

For the past two days we've been wrangling with an ASP.NET issue. We have a page that takes JSON, deserializes with the the System.Web.Extensions.dll JSON serializer stuff, and then serializes a response back to the client. For some reason, the first time we do this, it takes 15 seconds for the call to complete, and we can't figure out why. So we've opened a support call with Microsoft, and the support guy sent me an e-mail detailing the steps he wanted me to take to get the debug dump files. I loved the title to his e-mail:

> Steps to take manual dump

It's a Friday :).
