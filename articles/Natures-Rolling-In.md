# Nature's Rolling In

I'm working on my code for my presentation I'm doing with Mike at the next Code Camp (BTW an initial session list has been posted). I'm about to go to bed, but I keep seeing a flicker of light out of the corner of my right eye. I look out my kitchen window (which is to my right) and look west. I can see flashes of lightening and as I type they're getting brighter and brighter with increasing frequency.

Kind of peaceful, really.

It's odd, though, that nothing is showing up on radar (I love weather.com these days, the Virtual Earth map combination with radar is amazing!).
