# Working From Home, Here I Come

Rob mentions the 4$/gallon for gas that's coming. C'mon, you know it's going to get that high, if not higher, in the very near future. Personally, I hope this pushes companies to start letting their employees work from home. Even if it's just 2 or 3 days a week, I'm guessing that has to help out a lot of folks not feel that slight pinch at the gas pump.
