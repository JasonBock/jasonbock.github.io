# Procrastination

Next Wednesday I'm giving an internal presentation to a company in the Twin Cities. This will probably come out all wrong, but I'll be glad when it's over. Part of it is the weather is getting nicer out with every passing day, and the last thing I want to do is stay in at put together a presentation. Part of it is that I have to put it together. I've done talks on this before (security in .NET) but I haven't done it in a couple of years and I need to revamp my approach. I have a talk in June and in August but that's giving my "Reflection in .NET" talk, and I've done that twice in the last two months so that material is pretty solid.

Once May 16th has come and gone I'll have a huge weight come off of my shoulders and I'll be looking forward to enjoying my summer.
