# I Apologize

Right now, someone is trying to convert me to their religious beliefs. I don't know the person personally, but I do know he reads my blog (so he'll probably read this too). Frankly, it's annoying. I'm quite happy with my current state of mind with respect to religious belief and I don't need someone slamming their beliefs down my throat ...

But as I was thinking about this, I remembered I used to do this to people all the time.

If you went to Marquette between 1989 and 1992, I may have tried to read some dumb tract to you from Campus Crusade for Christ.

If you went to Daytona Beach for Spring Break in either 1989 or 1990, I was combing the beach looking for lost souls to convert.

Basically, I was kind of annoying for a couple of years in my life [1].

So this post is an official apology to those that I tried to convert. Sorry if I annoyed you.

[1] Some people may say that I'm **still** annoying. But that's for other reasons ;).
