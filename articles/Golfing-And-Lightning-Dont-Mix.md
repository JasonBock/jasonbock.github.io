# Golfing and Lightning Don't Mix

No, I didn't get hit today, but it got really bad around the 4th hole [1], so I hitched a ride on the cart the two guys I was playing with and we high-tailed it for the clubhouse. I waited for a bit, but after a while I gave up and reschedule my tee time for Saturday morning. Now I'm going to play (weather permitting) Thursday, Saturday and Sunday. Not that I'm complaining or anything ...

[1] I wonder if God didn't want me to play because I started 6, 6, 6. Because, you know, that whole numerology thing works.
