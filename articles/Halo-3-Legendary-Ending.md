# Halo 3 Legendary Ending

Don't watch [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yygn_vlH5a4) (or keep reading) if you haven't finished the game - spoilers abound! It shows the ending with Master Chief alive and going into statis, which is a fitting ending as you start Halo coming out of statis. But if you play the game on Legendary, you get a brief scene after the ending that shows the trilogy of games is done ... but something else is probably going to start up ...

This ending doesn't surprise me one bit. There's no way Microsoft is going to let Halo/Master Chief just fade away - it's just too much of a cash cow. And there's still 6 Halos floating around in the galaxy. Personally, I'm kind of interested to see where things go from here.
