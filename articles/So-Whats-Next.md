# So What's Next?

That means figuring out a weekend when I can coerce friends to help, getting the materials, etc. Fortunately I found out yesterday that a neighbor builds decks on the side, and while I'm not going to have him do it, I hope I can have him come over and give me a tour of what it takes to build a deck.

It'll be a fair amount of money, but I don't get to see my sister and her family (who live in Oregon) that often so I'm really looking forward to it.

My Quixo project is the first one on the list - there's a couple of internal changes that I want to make, but there's another reason I want to get the upgrades done ... somebody wants to build a fancy UI above it. Details will be coming whenever I get done and then this person decides to build the UI ... so don't hold your breath.

Oh, and of course, above all, enjoy being with Liz and Hayden.
