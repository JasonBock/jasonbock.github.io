# Vintage Keyboard: Roland Juno 60

Today I got to talking with a fellow Magenicon about music, which lead to keyboardists, which led to getting nostalgic about my old Roland Juno 60. You can find out more about this keyboard [here](https://www.vintagesynth.com/roland/juno-60). I'll never give this up (unless I get a ton of money for it). You can only play 6 notes on it at a time, but that analog sound is hard to beat. It's so smooth and warm. I played "Jump" on that thing when I was in high school - it was awesome!
