# PGR3 Tourney: Even Closer

Last night I cut my time down to 54:21, and the cutoff was at 54:05 - I moved up to 99th place. So I've been able to shave just over a second from a couple of days ago, but it's getting harder to knock of those fractions of a second. I think the final cutoff will be around 53:90 - I doubt it'll stay in the 54 second world for long.
