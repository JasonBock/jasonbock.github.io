# GA/EP Material

I've mentioned before that I'm interested in learning more about evolutionary programming and genetic algorithms. I looked through the books I have and I've decided to go through these:

* An Introduction to Genetic Algorithms
* Evolutionary Computation 1: Basic Algorithms and Operators
* Practical Genetic Algorithms
* Creative Evolutionary Systems
* Genetic Algorithms in Search, Optimization, and Machine Learning
* Evolutionary Computation: Towards a New Philosophy of Machine Intelligence
* Neural Smithing
* Evolutionary Computation 2: Advanced Algorithms and Operators
* Evolutionary Algorithms
* Evolutionary Computation in Bioinformatics

I realize there may be better books out there but this is what was lying around.

I have no idea how long it's going to take me to get through all this. 6 months? 6 years? We'll see ... but I'll never make any progress unless I get started. To discipline myself better, I'll post reviews of the material I've read - that'll help me remember what I've gone over.
