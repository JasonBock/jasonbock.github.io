# Queensryche and Ronnie James Dio

For Queensryche's next album, "Operation: Mindcrime II", Ronnie James Dio is singing the part for Dr. X. That should be very interesting. The art for the CD is up on their site - you can see it here. Queensryche's last 3 CD efforts have been less than impressive - it'll be interesting to see what they do with this. "Operation: Mindcrime" is one of the best rock CDs in history, but I have to wonder if Queensryche is using that fact to try and squeeze some profits out of a sequel by releasing a subpar collection of songs, or if this CD will be a logical extension of the first one. March 14th, 2006 will reveal all ...
