# Louder is Not Better

Before "Snakes and Arrows" Rush released "Vapor Trails". Musically, I love that CD, but when you listen to it, you may notice that it sounds "harsh". This site going into some details about how VT was really wrecked from a listening standpoint.

Of course, requests/pleas have been coming from Rush fans to remaster this CD because, well, it does sound like crap. Now there's a petition you can sign, which probably won't do any good, but hey, I signed it!
