# "Fallen Dragon", by Peter Hamilton

I finished this book Monday night after months of chipping away at its' fairly massive size. All in all, it's an OK sci-fi novel, but I didn't enjoy it as much as his "Reality Dysfunction" series. That set of books is worth the time you invest in reading it. This book ... well, I wasn't disappointed, but I wasn't satisfied either.
