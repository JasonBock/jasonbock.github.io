# Two Days of Hell

Today Liz and I took the boys to the Cracker Barrel in Lakeville for Mother's Day. I think Liz doesn't get enough credit for raising our boys. It's definitely stressful for her during the day, but she is doing a fantastic job and I feel fortunate that she's such a good mother.

Unfortunately, I've been procrastinating getting my material together for my talk on Wednesday. I actually got my slides together this afternoon; there really isn't much there, as I'm getting away from having a lot of slides and focusing more on the code. I know which samples I want to present, but I have to get all of them together by Tuesday night. I can't wait for Wednesday to roll around - that will mark the beginning of summer for me.
