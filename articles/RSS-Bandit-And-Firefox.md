# RSS Bandit and Firefox

Well, after playing with these two applications, I can say that I like both of them. However, RSS Bandit has definitely replaced SharpReader as my blogreader, whereas Firefox hasn't "wowwed" me. It's not that it's bad; there's some things about Firefox that like over IE. But, really, there isn't that much about browsers that wow me anymore. My Internet experience has really changed over the last couple of years. I just don't surf much anymore. The browser itself is just not that big of a deal. I can deal with both IE and Firefox.

So, both tools get a thumbs-up in my book. But only one will become part of my day-to-day activities.
