# Traveling to Milwaukee

I leave for tomorrow for a 2-day stint in downtown Milwaukee. This **may** lead to a longer gig if things work out. While I don't like traveling, going to a city that I know extremely well is not all that bad. Plus, if this works out I'd be down there for SummerFest, which is a great party with great music on the shores of Lake Michigan. And I have a lot of family and friends in the Milwaukee area, so it'll be fun to meet up with people I haven't seen in a while.
