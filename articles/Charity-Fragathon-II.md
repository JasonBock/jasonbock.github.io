# Charity Fragathon II

This past weekend Chris's event took place. We had 39 people in the Halo3:ODST tournament, with about 50 people overall showing up. And the best thing is that we raised $1,000 and lots of canned goods for Second Harvest Food Bank. Congratulations goes out to the top 3 in the tournament:

* David Engler
* Jesse Larson
* Matt Carlson

I'm sure there will be a CF3 in the future, probably some time after Halo:Reach is released (but you'd have to ask Chris about that :) ).
