# Interesting Neil Peart News

How I wish Neil would add a RSS feed to his site. Unfortunately, he doesn't have one yet, but I noticed that there's some new information about his upcoming projects. This includes a new book on the R30 tour and an new instructional DVD (yes!). I especially love his description of a sporting event (remember that he says he doesn't know a lot about sports):

> Some guy took some performance-enhancing drugs and hit a triple into the end zone during the fourth quarter with a four under par, but was whistled down for interference. Then there was a fight.

Sounds like he actually knows a lot about sports to know all of those terms!
