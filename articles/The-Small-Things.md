# The Small Things

I've been meaning to post an update on the boys, but I wanted to share this story before I go to bed.

Yesterday we had the craving for Jimmy John's for dinner, so Hayden went along with me. That way he could help me out by carrying the bag of sandwiches back to the car.

We get into the facility, and we wait in line.

Then I notice something. Hayden is standing next to me, and he ever so slowly leans into me. It's not sudden and it's not a jerky movement ... it's subtle.

The place was kind of loud for him ... so he wanted to be close to Dad to get a little reassurance.

I really can't describe what I felt at that moment.

Sometimes it's the small things that make the most impact.
