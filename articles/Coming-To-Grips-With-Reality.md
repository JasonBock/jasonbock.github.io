# Coming to Grips With Reality

For a while now, I've been lamenting about my weight. I'm overweight. I know it. I can feel it. But, to be honest, I really haven't tried to change it.

That changes today.

On my Twitter account, every morning, I'm going to tweet my weight. This sounds simple, but the point is, putting that out in the open will make me feel like I'm keeping myself accountable. No one else will probably care (or at least they shouldn't) but I know that I'm doing it, and that's what matters.

Oh, and I'm sure to get lots of twitter spam via followers. I'm used to it, I don't really care :)

My goal? Lose 30 pounds by the end of this year, if not quicker. Since my first weigh-in was 230, that means hitting 200 by the end of this year.

I **will** do it.
