# 10 Years Today

Today marks the 10-year anniversary of my marriage to Liz. It really hasn't felt like it's been that long. I feel very fortunate that I found someone that I get along with so easy. Yesterday I went golfing with someone from work and at the 19th hole we were talking about marriage, kids, etc. I told him that Liz is my best friend, and I realized that's what it takes to have a marriage that will work. I don't really look at Liz being my wife without looking at her and thinking she's so much more than that.
