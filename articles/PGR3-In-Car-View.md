# PGR3 In-Car View

PGR3 has a new in-car view that's pretty sweet. The detail is amazing, but I've never liked in-car views when I play racing games because I can't see what on each side of my car. However, hosts can set up the game to force members of the room to use a certain car view or to use manual. I was in a room last night that forced in-car view, and ... man, it's intense. Feels like driving go-karts around. You definitely get a good feel for driving the car, that's for sure.
