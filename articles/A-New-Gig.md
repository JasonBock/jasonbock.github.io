# A New Gig

Today I started a 3-month gig at a client where I don't think I'm going to write one line of code while I'm there. This gig is going to be completely different from anything I've ever done - I'm basically there to help their team leads work with a new internal software development process they've created (which really isn't all that revolutionary, but they need to follow a process as they don't have anything right now). The process also needs to be evolved, so I'll try to throw my agile mindset in whenever I can. I'll keep you updated as to how this gig fans out. It may be the most boring 3 months of my life, or they may be very educational.
