# "Snakes And Arrows" - Decision

After playing the music over, and over ... and over, I've come to this conclusion.

This is Rush's best CD ever.

I love the mix. I love the music (OK, "Bravest Face" isn't clicking with me just yet, but you can't please everyone). The lyrics are mature and relevant. The arrangements are not so much complex as they are well-structured yet loose. The key thing is the songs are **sticking**. I'm remembering the stuff very easily.

This is by far one of the best CDs I've heard in a while.
