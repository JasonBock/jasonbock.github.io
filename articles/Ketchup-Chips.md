# Ketchup Chips

Earlier this week my Canadian co-worker Marc brought some ketchup chips in for us to try:

![Ketchup Chips](https://jasonbock.net/images/Ketchup-Chips.png "Ketchup Chips")

Apparently ketchup chips are something Canadians go ga-ga over. I've never heard of them, but I though, what the hell.

Unfortunately, I didn't groove on them. The Old Dutch weren't bad, but the others were way too sweet. The Pringles were awful, which were surprising, because I **love** Pringles.
