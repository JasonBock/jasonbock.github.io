# The Sarah Conner Chronicles

Looks like the Terminator series is coming to TV. I have a really bad feeling about this. It doesn't seem to follow anything from T3. Sarah is dead in T3, and John doesn't mention anything about some female terminator/whatever-the-hell that hottie is in her class that protects him in his past. Maybe it's just a continuation from T2. I had read the T2 books and the "Infiltrator" one mentions humans that were modified to be terminators - maybe this series uses that idea in a "good" way.

I just can't watch anyone playing Sarah other than Linda Hamilton - she **made** that character (especially in T2). I'll probably watch this for a little while ... but I really can't see anything good coming from it.
