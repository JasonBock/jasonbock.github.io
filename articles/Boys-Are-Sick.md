# Boys are Sick

I guess both Hayden and Ryan have the flu to some degree. So far it's the "typical" flu stuff, so, yeah, it's the pukey kind ... but so far it's not **really** bad.

I hate the flu.

Until we had kids ... well, I can't remember the last time I had the flu - I think I was 24 or 25. Sure, I got colds and headaches and whatnot, but I'm like Seinfeld - I hate throwing up. Not like anyone does, mind you, but the flu is just ... ugh!

Admittedly, having kids had hardened me up to the grossities of life. But it doesn't mean I like dealing with it either. Such is the territory of being a parent. I just don't want to get it. I can deal with feeling achy and dizzy and other flu symptoms, but puking I can't stand.

Sheesh, just writing about it is making me "blah".
