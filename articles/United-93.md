# "United 93"

Buy it. Watch it. Borrow it. Do whatever you can do to watch this movie. It's not fun. It's gut-wrenching. But it's more than worth it to see this depiction of what the passengers did to try and thwarte the 4th and final 9/11 attack. This is by far one of the best movies I've ever seen. It makes no attempt to make the situations and circumstances anything more or less than what they were. It makes an amazing, slow build of tension until the chaotic end, and it's beyond emotional. I don't think awards dictate the worth of a movie, but this one better win Best Picture. It deserves it more than any other movie I've seen (and I'm a big fan of "The Shawshank Redemption" so that should tell you something).
