# Mike ... Recording!

![Mike - Recording](https://jasonbock.net/images/Mike-Recording.png "Mike - Recording")

To make a long story short, Mike and I were able to work on some Sidetracked Project songs at his pad last night. For the last 8 months, we've been working solo, but we decided to work together as he worked on the vocal tracks to "Scared" and "Who I Am". It went really well. I think giving Mike another pair of ears helped him get through the parts quicker. All that's left for him to do vocal-wise is tweak some stuff on "Pushed" and then the cover of Iron Maiden's "Aces High". I wish I could see him try to sing that one.

I keep saying it, but we're really close to being done. Hopefully by this summer we'll have the final mix complete.
