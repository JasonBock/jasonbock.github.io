# Stressed and Excited About TCCC 2

There's only a couple of days left before the 2nd TCCC kicks off. I'm a bit stressed - lots of little things to make sure are correct, and I have to practice my talk tonight, and at the client, I'm finishing an interation this week that's pretty critical for the product. At the same time, I'm really excited to get things going on Saturday. The registration numbers are much higher than the first one. I think the format will be better than last time (addition of chalk talks plus more time for the sessions). And the prizes are kick-ass.

I'm sure I'll be going out for some drinks afterwards!
