# One Thing I've Always Wondered About the "Cars" Movie

Having a son who loves cars and trucks, he likes watching the "Cars" movie. While it's not my favorite Pixar flick, I like the movie (the ending scene with McQueen pushing the King across the finish line always gets me). But one thing I've wondered is, who does the voice for the Arnold Schwarzenegger character? As far as I could tell, nobody is listed in the credits for doing the voice. Is it an imitator? Or ... is it Arnie himself, doing a really bad imitation of his own voice? I've done my share of searching and I can't find out who does the voice.  

Anybody know?
