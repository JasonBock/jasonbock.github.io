# "Rush: Chemistry", by Jon Collins

Being an avid Rush fan, I had to pick up this book. It was a decent read, but, again, I am a biased fan of the band so any material surrounding or about Geddy, Alex, and Neil I'll probably have a favorable view of it. The book has some minor abruptness to it as it tells the tale of the band's 30+ year history. However, it also has some decent behind-the-scenes stories about the members growing up and how each album/CD was made. If you're a Rush fan you'll probably get something out of it.
