# Teaching Java and Playing Golf

I've been teaching a Java course at Intertech for the past three days - hence the quiet lull on my site. It was a very interesting experience - it was my first course and I was expecting anything to happen. Fortunately, it went well and I'm looking forward to doing it again.

Last Sunday I went golfing at Valley View. I shot a 45 on the front, but through the first five holes on the back nine, I was 1-over par. So I'm thinking, this is one of the best rounds of my life! Unfortunately, as soon as I thought that, the double-bogeys came raining down on my parade, and I ended up with a 47. Oh, well - I still had fun. I played nine holes last night at a fairly easy course and I shot a 41 - go figure.
