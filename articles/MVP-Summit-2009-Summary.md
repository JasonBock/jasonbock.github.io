# MVP Summit 2009 Summary

Well, the event is over, and for me, it was pretty much a bust. Not because of the event **itself**, mind you, but being sick prevented me from being as social as I wanted to. A big part of having a personally successful MVP Summit is to be social: meet people on the product teams, get to know other MVPs, etc. I really had no energy or desire to do that. I'd get back to the hotel early evening and the only thing I wanted to do was to sit in bed and go to sleep. It sucked, but at least I was able to get some things out of the sessions that I did attend.

Hopefully next year I won't get a sinus infection and things will go much better.
