# Travels so Far

Since Wednesday night I've been on the road. I did two sessions at VSLive on Thursday, and then I flew up to Seattle on Saturday for the MVP Summit. I did a talk yesterday called "Evolving LINQ Expressions". Someone took a picture of me when I did the talk, purple shirt and all:

![MVP Summit 2009](https://jasonbock.net/images/MVP-Summit-2009.png "MVP Summit 2009")

That talk seemed well-received. I spent more time on the Powerpoint than any other session in my life. I can't post the material right now due to network restrictions but I'll do that as soon as I can.

I wish I could say that I've been having a good time ... well, I **am**, but I've been fighting a very bad cold/fever since I took off on Wednesday and I just cannot shake it. I've been debating if I should see a doctor out there - I'm wondering if it's something potentially more serious. It's hard to say. But I haven't been been going to any late-night events and I haven't been able to socialize as much as I want to. I've been able to meet some people here and there but it's hard to be social when I feel like crap.
