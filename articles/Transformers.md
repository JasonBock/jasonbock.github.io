# Transformers

Liz and I saw this movie on Saturday. It is about the dumbest, most ridiculous sci-fi movies I've ever seen, and I didn't care one bit when I was watching it. If you see it, don't even try to critically analyze it, because there are so many gaping holes in the plot and the storyline in general it's not even funny. Just suspend your logic for 2 1/2 hours and enjoy some unbelievable special effects (the end fight scene is just amazing)
