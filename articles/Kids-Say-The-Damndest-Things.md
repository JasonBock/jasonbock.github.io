# Kids Say The Damndest Things

Yesterday at dinner Hayden dropped his spoon on my shorts, and it was covered with pudding. Hayden's always been good with his utensils, but it's been tradition for him to eat the pudding out of the cup himself and then he hands it to either Liz or I. I wasn't expecting it and the spoon fell out of the cup, staining my pants.

It wasn't a big deal, but for some reason I let go with a "damnit!"

Of course, the next thing out of Hayden's mouth is, "damnit!"

I had to leave the kitchen - I was laughing too hard and I didn't want him to think that saying that word was acceptable/funny :).

Live and learn.
