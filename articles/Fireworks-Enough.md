# Fireworks ... Enough!

OK, I know tomorrow's a big day for families in our neighborhood to do their own fireworks display. They load up on (probably illegal) explosive devices of every kind and light them all off. I don't mind that; in fact, I usually go over to the neighbor's houses and watch the displays. But what I have a big issue with is some of our neighbors blowing shit up for the past four fracking nights around 10 PM that's concussive enough to shake our windows.

Yes, the cops were called last night.

And I'll call them again tonight if those asshats do it again.

My kids are like me in that they can sleep through anything. But for FSM's sake, I don't need a fireworks display every night 4 days before and after the 4th. Knock it off. It's annoying, it's disrespectful, and I'm old and crotchety and I don't want to hear that crap.
