# Rusty Drumming

Yesterday I got home and Liz had the kids over at the neighbors. Before I went over there, I used the "alone" time to jump on my drum set and play a bit.

Wow ... am I **rusty**!

I need to get the pads on the set and play more. Drumming isn't my first instrument and I haven't played in nearly a year, and it felt like it.
