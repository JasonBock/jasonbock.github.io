# Las Vegas

Now that I have some time, here's a brief synopsis of our 10-year anniversary celebration in Las Vegas.

We flew in late Thursday night, and we got into our room at the MGM Grand:

![Las Vegas - MGM Grand](https://jasonbock.net/images/Las-Vegas-MGM.png "Las Vegas - MGM Grand")

We had a great view of the Hooters hotel out our window:

![Las Vegas - Hooters](https://jasonbock.net/images/Las-Vegas-Hooters.png "Las Vegas - Hooters")

We spent a lot of time relaxing, drinking, eating, and gambling. We spent most of our time around the machines where you could win a Dodge Viper:

![Las Vegas - Gambling](https://jasonbock.net/images/Las-Vegas-Gambling.png "Las Vegas - Gambling")

![Las Vegas - Dodge Viper](https://jasonbock.net/images/Las-Vegas-Viper.png "Las Vegas - Dodge Viper")

No, we didn't win it, but we had lots of fun playing the slots.

We spent some time Saturday night walking around the strip. We stopped by the Bellagio - I've always wanted to see the water show:

![Las Vegas - Bellagio](https://jasonbock.net/images/Las-Vegas-Bellagio.png "Las Vegas - Bellagio")

Overall, we had a lot of fun. I'm not a big fan of huge crowds so I think only being out there for a couple of days was a perfect amount of time:

![Las Vegas - Us](https://jasonbock.net/images/Las-Vegas-Us.png "Las Vegas - Us")

And yes, what happens in Vegas, stays in Vegas :).
