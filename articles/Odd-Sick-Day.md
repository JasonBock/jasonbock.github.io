# Odd Sick Day

Today was the day I was going to give my security presentation, but as I was getting ready for work I could clearly tell Liz was very sick. I debated a bit whether or not I should cancel the talk ... and then I started to feel a little sick. I didn't get it as bad as she did (and Liz started getting better around noon - she's up and about right now), but I got one of the worst headaches I ever did in the afternoon. I felt really bad cancelling my talk, but I made the right choice. We were fortunate to find someone who could watch the kids today - Megan, you're a lifesaver!

Now we're just worried that Hayden and/or Ryan will get this ...
