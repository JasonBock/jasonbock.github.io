# Gas is Getting Out of Control Again

For the past couple of months, gas prices have been rising ... and rising ... and rising. Around my neck of the woods, it's up to $2.80 (more or less).

Yikes.

Methinks we desperately need a Manhattan Project-like ... project to get us away from fossil fuels (and that won't end up creating a weapon that's even worse than a nuclear bomb).
