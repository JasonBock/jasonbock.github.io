# Lack of Blogging

I'm going down the path that is somewhat typical these days: my blogging is fading. Twitter has been a part of it: it's easier to do little mind dumps in that space than on my blog. I'm also busier with family and job activites and don't have a lot of energy to blog longer pieces. I also know that my weight is part of the lack of energy - I need to lose weight to get some of that energy back.

I do have some ideas for blog posts in mind in the future. I also don't think that blogging is "dead" - I believe there is a place for it. But as social networks evolve, it's clear that blogs are finding their place, and it seems to me that they're not as widespread as they used to be. They're still relevant, but there are other ways to state what's on your mind.
