# Loving my New Phone

I am in love with my new phone (XV6800). I got e-mail integrated on it along with my calendar, I have an unlimited Internet plan - it's awesome. I was checking the status of the flight yesterday and I knew what the new delay time was before it showed up on the screen behind the gate. The power ... the power ...

And someone did find my old phone. That's the downfall of Magenic getting us really nice backpacks that look exactly the same. I accidentally put my phone in someone else's bag Tuesday night and he found it on Saturday. Oh well, I got all the rebates on the new phone and I was going to get it anyway this Saturday.
