# "Lost" Has End-Date

This is good news. I'm glad they did this, because this prevents the story from spinning off into la-la land (ala "The X-Files"). I've always thought the story has a core conclusion in mind, but this beginning of this season was starting to make me wonder. The last 4 or 5 episodes have definitely picked things up and I'm really enjoying the season as a whole (unlike "24" which totally sucks this year).
