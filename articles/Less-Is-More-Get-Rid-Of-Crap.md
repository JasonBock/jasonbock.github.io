# Less is More, Get Rid Of Crap

I love [this post](https://www.pickthebrain.com/blog/living-with-less-8-reasons-to-embrace-the-simple-life/). Liz and I have started to dump a lot of crap out of our house. Going through all of my books will be hard...but I need to get rid of some of them as well. We're making good progress and we can do even better. I agree, less is more.

I've always found it funny when some people we know have made comments that our home is our starter home and when we buy our bigger home, blah, blah, blah. There's no reason for us to get a bigger home. It suits us just fine. We have a hard enough time keeping this one clean!
