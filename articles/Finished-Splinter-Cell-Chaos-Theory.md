# Finished "Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory"

This game was just like the first two, which means I really liked it. The ending was a bit anti-climatic. The first one was good because you had to run out of a building guns ablaze, and the second one's last confrontation took a long time to figure out (although the animated ending was weak). This just kind of "ended", which I didn't like. But it was worth the money. I haven't played it online much - the co-op is cool but it's unstable. I've experienced a lot of room disconnects with it. Good thing I finished yesterday as Forza is coming out tomorrow - w00t!
