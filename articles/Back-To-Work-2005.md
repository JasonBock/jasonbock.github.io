# Back To Work

Well, my vacation's over, and tomorrow I go back to work. Vacations always go by way too fast, no matter how hard I try to slow them down so I can enjoy them. I forgot to play the Powerball this week - it was pretty high on Wednesday. I got used to just being able to do what I wanted to, when I wanted to. Oh, sure, who wouldn't want that? All I'm saying is that I don't want my vacation to end, but I'm also looking forward to getting back at the keyboard (on my laptop, that is) and bashing out code again. I don't notice how much vacations recharge the batteries until I get back to development, so we'll see how this week goes.
