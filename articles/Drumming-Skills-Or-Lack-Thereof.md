# Drumming Skills (or Lack Thereof)

Last night I spent some time on my drum set. I haven't played them in a while because ... they're drums and they're loud and we have a split level so there's no door to shut so Hayden wouldn't get an earful. I have pads but it's just not the same thing. Anyway, I played for a while yesterday, and man, I am so out of practice. The big thing is my right hand feels like it's cramping up a bit - it's not used to the abuse. At least I have a whole week to whip it back into shape.
