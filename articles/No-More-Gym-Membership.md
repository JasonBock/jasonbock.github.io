# No More Gym Membership

Friday I canceled our gym membership.

It's weird. I've been going to the gym now since my college days at least 2 times a week, if not more. But this summer my love for biking has been rekindled, and it's just so much easier to hop on the bike and go for 30-40 minutes. Granted, being in MN it's going to get colder (eventually) but Saturday I was getting my bike fixed and I was looking at a couple of devices that you can hook your bike up to so you can use it indoors. Perfect - I'm going to get one in a month or so. I'm also looking at getting some free weights to keep some strength in my upper body.

This isn't a statement that gyms suck. Far from it. It's just that right now (and probably for the foreseeable future) it's far easier to get the workouts done at home.
