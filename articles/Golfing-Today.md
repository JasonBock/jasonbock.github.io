# Golfing Today

Today I went golfing to New Prague Golf Course. I shot 44-44 for an 88, which suprised the hell out of me. Maybe I shouldn't play that much - I seem to do better that way. My short game was kind of crappy and my irons were coming up a bit short. My woods [1] were going pretty good though and I got the putting feel after a couple of holes.

[1] They're not "woods" anymore, but calling them "metals" seems odd as well.
