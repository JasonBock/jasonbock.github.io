# Another Gig Has Ended

Yesterday I finished up my latest gig. Initially, it was a disaster - way too much work to do with an extremely short schedule, and most of the work was based on a technology I have no experience with. Fortunately, we were able to find another firm that specializes in that techology, and I could focus on the .NET coding side of things. It's been nice because I've been able to work from home, but while the work wasn't bad it was fairly straightforward stuff. I'm interviewing for a new gig on Friday - tech lead/architect role for at least a year and the commute is pretty nice, so we'll see how that goes.
