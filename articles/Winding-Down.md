# Winding Down

I'm finally coming off what was probably the busiest time period of my life. The last 4 to 6 months have been very stressful to say the least.

I've been working on a project for a client that has required a lot of overtime. It's a long story as to why that happened, but the end result is I was working a **lot** of nights and weekends. Sure, one can say that I should be thankful that I have a job at all (and I am), but when I heard my sons saying, "why is Dad on the computer so much" ... well, that got to me. Thankfully, the project is done today, and other than the long hours, it's been a fun project.

The long hours also didn't help in me finishing up the "Metaprogramming in .NET" book, but I can finally say that **all** of the material is done and it's in the production stage. I can't wait until I get the book in my hands, which should be late this year.

I also had to get the roof replaced on my house, and that was a bill I didn't really want. But that was finalized with the last payment a couple of weeks ago. By the way, if you need a new roof and you live in the Twin Cities are, or you need other house work done, contact Intelligent Designs. They were great to work with and I wouldn't hesitate to use them again.

All in all, I'm looking forward to taking 2 weeks off in October :). I'm starting a new gig up with Magenic next Monday as well. I'm also (hopefully) speaking at a couple of code camps in October and November, and I'm also speaking at VSLive in Orlando in December. On the musical side of my life, Mike and I have some songs written for the Sidetracked Project and we're looking to start recording them over the last months of 2012.

And I should really update my custom blog engine ... if I can find the time!
