# Tomorrow Will (Hopefully) be a Good Day

There are two reasons I think I'll be happy tomorrow:

* My 2 weeks of continuous travel will be over and I can sleep in my own bed for more than two days.
* Liz will be 18 weeks into her pregnancy. [1]

'nuff said.

[1] I'm really not superstitious, but ... knock on wood, cross fingers, pray to Cthulhu, walk under a ladder ... any juju helps ;) I'm surpised I haven't become a raging alcoholic with all of the worry and concern I'm supressing.
