# A Relaxing Weekend

I dropped Liz off at the airport an hour ago. It was nice having her out in California for a couple of days. We went to have a good Italian dinner Friday night at the Ristorante Don Giovanni in Mountain View. On Saturday, we drove down to Monterey and went to the Aquarium. While marine life isn't something I'm well-versed in, it was very interesting watching the fish swim by you in these huge tanks. It was well worth the trip.
