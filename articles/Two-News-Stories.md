# Two News Stories

OK, one story about Christmas. Sunday Liz and I were completely oblivious to news-related events. So, by the time we got to my Mom's house, she said, "Did you hear about Reggie White dying?" It suprised the hell out of me, so I turned on the TV, and ... well, my heart just sank after I heard about Asia. Granted, Reggie was a great guy to have in Green Bay - he turned the Packers around and he died way too young. But, when you hear about more than **20,000** people dying in a matter of seconds ... there's just no way to compare it. That's astoundingly sad. I feel for White's family, but the magnitude of death and destruction in southeast Asia is catastrophic.

I can't even find words to describe how terrible of an event that was.
