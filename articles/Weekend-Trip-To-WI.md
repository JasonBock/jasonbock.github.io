# Weekend Trip to WI

This weekend I drove down to WI myself to pick up some of my Dad's tools. It was nice having two long drives - I needed some alone time. I stopped by my friend Tim's house Saturday night and caught up with him (and remembered the good old times ... like when we had long hair). But probably the best part about the whole trip was when I stopped for lunch in Chippewa Falls, WI, and saw an oil change and car wash place called the (wait for it) "Lube and Wash".

I so wish I had a camera along to take a picture of that sign!
