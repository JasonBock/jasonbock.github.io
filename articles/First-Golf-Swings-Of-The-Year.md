# First Golf Swings of the Year

This morning I got 9 holes of golf in. I haven't hit a golf ball in nearly 6 months, and I didn't have time to hit the driving range before today, so I expected things to be a bit rough. They were ;). Actually, it wasn't as bad as I thought it could be - I hit some decent drives and a couple nice fairway woods. But I didn't break 50, and that's usually a indicator of a decent round for me. Oh, well, things can only get better.
