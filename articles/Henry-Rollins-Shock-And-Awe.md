# Henry Rollins: "Shock and Awe"

Liz and I watched Henry's video this weekend. **Highly** recommended. I have to see Henry live in person at least once in my life. He's a great storyteller - the bit about the guy breaking into his house is fu-u-ny. The safety helmet, signing the bomb ... all worth watching.

He also mentions a day recording a song with (wait for it) William Shatner and Adrian Belew. It's called, "I Can't Get Behind That", and sure enough, the Zune marketplace had it. Some funny, funny shit right there.
