# Completely Drained

Yesterday we had the carpets stretched and I cleaned them. Then I started moving all the furniture back to their correct spots. I got everything upstairs done and around 8:30 PM I said, "that's it, I'm done." I was dead on my feet. I could barely lift my ass off the couch, let alone move more furniture. Now I feel better so it's off to the downstairs to finish it off.

By the way I'm dumping a fair amount of books to a local Half-Price Books, probably the one in Burnsville. I'm getting rid of some old computer books and religious material so if you're really interested in learning about ASP or you love fundamentalism, check it out in a couple of days.

And just to reiterate (because Mike can't read), **I am not moving**.
