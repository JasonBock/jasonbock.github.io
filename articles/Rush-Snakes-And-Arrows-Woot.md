# Rush: "Snakes and Arrows" - WOOT!!!!

It's official - Rush's new CD is called "Snakes and Arrows" and there's a **very** brief clip of their first single, "Far Cry". If you're a die-hard Rush fanatic (like I am), you'll recognize that F#-based chord that's prevalent in "Hemispheres". I can't wait to hear more, and I gotta start my usual tradition of listening to all their CDs from the first all the way to "R30".
