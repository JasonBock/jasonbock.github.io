# "Reversing"

I finished this book a couple of nights ago. It's very well-written and thorough. It shows you the details you need to understand how people can reverse programs and understand how they work along with the countermeasures developers take to thwart reversing. The chapters where he shows programs that encrypt their x86 assembly was very interesting. If you're interested in reverse engineering of software programs this is a good book to get.
