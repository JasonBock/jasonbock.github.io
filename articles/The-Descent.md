# "The Descent"

Wow.

This movie is amazingly good. Be warned, it's bloody and gory as hell sometimes, and pretty frightening. But there's also some subtle, cool underlying concepts going on in the movie (don't worry, I won't give away any spoilers). I saw the unrated version - I heard the ending in the theaters was "different" (again, I won't give away how it's different). Personally, I liked the way the unrated version ended. Overall, if you want a good, decent, horror movie, don't hesitate to rent/buy this. It's well worth it.
