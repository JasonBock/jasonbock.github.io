# Voting

I just voted. It's the first time in my life that I voted. And ... what the hell is the big deal? You fill in some ovals, you feed it into a machine - what could be easier?

No, I didn't get one of the "I Voted" stickers.

Yes, I did vote for Kerry. Bush-lovers are free to blame me for all of society's ills, even if Kerry doesn't win.

And no, P-Diddy wasn't waiting to pop a cap in my ass if I didn't vote (because, otherwise ... **I'D DIE**).
