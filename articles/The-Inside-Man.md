# "The Inside Man"

Liz and I rented "The Inside Man" last night. I really enjoyed this movie. It wasn't your standard bank robbery movie, and even though you know there's something deeper going on in the script, the ending twist is pretty cool. I didn't see it coming, although I'm usually hit-and-miss when it comes to movies like this. In hindsight they really give away a big clue when they describe the hole they're digging as a "shit-hole", but it didn't dawn on me what they were getting at until the end (I won't give it completely away, though). It's definitely worth your time to watch this movie. It's entertaining and well-thought out.
