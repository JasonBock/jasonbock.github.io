# First Pregnancy News

Well, the "NDA/good news" I mentioned a couple of weeks ago was that Liz and I were pregnant. Unfortunately, Liz miscarried today. We were so excited to be on the road to being parents, so it's been a hard, emotional day. Fortunately, Liz has sisters who have been through it before so they could console her, and the neighbors have been sympathetic as well.
