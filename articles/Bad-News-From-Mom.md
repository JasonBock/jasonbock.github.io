# Bad News From Mom

Bad news. I talked to my Mom last night, and she told me she has cancer (in her thyroid). Supposedly the outlook looks good right now, but I know my Mom - she doesn't like to tell me all of the details. One year after Dad dies of cancer, she's diagnosed with it.

All is not bad, though - I do have some good news to share, but it'll have to wait until Thanksgiving is over.
