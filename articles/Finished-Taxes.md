# Finished Taxes

I finished filing our taxes on Saturday. A couple of years ago, we finally decided to go the electronic route, but this was the first year I didn't actually purchase the software at a store. I did it all online. It's amazing to think that 10 years ago you either had to manually slog through a bunch of paperwork or have someone else do it. Now, I had everything completed and submitted in a hour. Nice!
