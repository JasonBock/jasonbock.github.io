# A Preview of Things to Come

Today we had a level 2 ultrasound. They took lots of pictures; unfortunately, none were of the digital kind. But there was a lot more detail in these scans than before, so I took a picture of the pictures, and...well, here you go:

![Preview of Things](https://jasonbock.net/images/Preview-Of-Things.png "Preview of Things")

A future angry coder is on the way ...

I admit, it may be hard to make out the details. The top picture is a side shot, and the bottom is a face.

To think I was a part of creating this is a little overwhelming. There's still 4 months of worry to go through, but so far everything looks good. I'm still not going to believe it until it happens, though ;).
