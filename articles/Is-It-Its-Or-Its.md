# Is It "It's", or "Its"?

Before I left for work this morning, I saw this text on CNN:

![It's or Its](https://jasonbock.net/images/Its.png "It's or Its")

I'm not good at English, but I think this is wrong - it should be "its".

I don't know why, but when I see people use "two", "too", and "to" incorrectly, or "their" or "they're", it annoys me. Really, I don't know why, but it does.

And to my current co-workers, yes, I now know that "irregardless" isn't a word. Bite me.
