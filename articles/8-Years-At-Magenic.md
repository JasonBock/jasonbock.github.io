# 8 Years At Magenic

Today marks my 8th year with Magenic. It's hard to believe that I've been with a company that long. Working for a consulting firm does have an advantage in that you end up working for a number of different companies so it really doesn't feel like you're doing the same thing all the time. But Magenic is also a great place to work, and that's why I've stuck around for as long as I have.

Oddly enough, I decided to wear the leather jacket I got for my 5th anniversary with Magenic today. And what do I get if I stick around for 10 years? A 3-month paid sabbatical. Yeah, that's something to stick around for!
