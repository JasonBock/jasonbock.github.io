# I'm Finally Free!

Today my college years are officially over. Well, I've been out of Marquette for a while now, but there's been one strong, legally binding chain that's been tugging on me ever since I left. But that chain has finally been cut - I made my last school loan payment! I now have no more academic debt. It's been seven years since I got my master's degree and acquired my first job, which legally forced me to hold up my end of the bargin. But now it's over. I am a **very** happy camper tonight! Wahoo!!!!!!
