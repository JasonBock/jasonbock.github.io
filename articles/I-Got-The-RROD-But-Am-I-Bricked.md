# I Got The RROD, But Am I Bricked?

For the past 2-3 weeks, my 360 has been acting a bit flakey. Bad internet connectivity ... odd, rare slowness in games ... I thought the RROD was coming, and sure enough, last night I got it. I fired it up this morning and all was well, so I'll keep trying it out until it's truly bricked before I send it in. Not that I need the distraction - I have slides to turn in by next Friday and I have to crank on that ...
