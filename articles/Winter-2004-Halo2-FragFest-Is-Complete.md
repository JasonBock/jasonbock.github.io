# Winter 2004 Halo2 FragFest is Complete

The final attendees packed up their TVs and left. It was **awesome**. Halo2 online is very cool, but getting people together to play, drink beer, and eat pizza is really nice. I had a tournament and a guy by the name of Phil Andunsen beat all comers (I used the time to videotape the events, so that's why I didn't win ... yeah, right!! ;) ).
