# On the Road for the Next Two Weeks

Well, my next gig requires me to travel for 2 weeks straight to Milwaukee. That means for the next two weeks I'll be in my own bed 2 times. I have to be honest, the timing on this isn't the greatest, but as I've said before, have laptop, will travel. The upshot to this gig is that after the initial two weeks I can work at home for the next two months, so that'll be nice :). But being away from home for 2 weeks will, quite frankly, suck.

Man, I don't know if I would've made it as a pioneer ;).
