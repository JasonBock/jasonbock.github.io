# "United 93" Is Not Nominated for Best Picture

I'm stunned. Completely stunned. "United 93" doesn't get a Best Picture nod, but "Little Miss Sunshine" (a film I saw and enjoyed) does?!

This is a farce. Yes, "United 93" is extremely difficult to watch. But it's worth it. It's one of the best movies I've ever seen. For it not get a Best Picture nomination is beyond stupid.

I just hope Greengrass gets Best Director ...
