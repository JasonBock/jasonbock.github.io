# "Resolution"

I finished this book last night. Very satisfying ending to the series. It seemed to tie in a lot of the plot points from the other two books in the series. I really like the main character, Tom. He's basically a kid in the first book who by chance (or is it?) gets a crystal from a Pilot, and from that point on his entire life changes in many way. All of these changes and adventures come to a wonderful conclusion at the end - I love the last thing he does. There's a book called "To Hold Infinity" that seems to exist in the same universe but earlier in time and on a different planet (the author also wrote it before the Nupalerion series) - I've already picked it up and I'll probably read that as well.
