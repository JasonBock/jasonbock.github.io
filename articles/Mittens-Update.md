# Mittens Update

Our new cat is doing just great in our household. For a while, he'd hiss at Tweak, but now they're best buddies. He's also incredibly sweet to the kids and very tolerant of their "handlings". Last night when I went to bed, he sat right next to my face and purred very loudly. We've been lucky with cats in the past and Mittens is no exception.
