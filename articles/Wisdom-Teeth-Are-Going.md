# Wisdom Teeth are Going

I went to the dentist today, and they said, "it's time to get rid of the wisdom teeth". They're not hurting or bothering me, but they said if I don't get rid of them I'll start to develop problems in the near future. So ... come early next year, I get to have the joy of having them brutally yanked out of my mouth ... one by bloody one.

I'm going to try and get this over with as soon as possible.
