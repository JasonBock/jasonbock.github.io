# Christians Boycott "The Golden Compass"?

Wow, this is more than enough reason there for me to want to watch it in the theater and recommend it to everyone I know ... even though I know very little about it! I remember when "The Last Temptation of Christ" came out, and some Christian groups got so damn vocal that they got it banned. That was atrocious. It's a great movie, and if it ruffles the feathers of some religious folk, who gives a rat's ass? That's the great thing about this country: if you don't like something, feel free to vent as much as you want and get others with the same mindset to boycott it, but damnit **let others make their own choices**. Fortuantely, it seems like they're not trying to get it banned, and frankly all they're really doing is generating more advertising for the movie.
