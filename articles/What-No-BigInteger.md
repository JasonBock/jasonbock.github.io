# What? No `BigInteger`?

One small yet nice addition to Orcas was `BigInteger`. Now I read that it's been removed. Does that mean it's gone completely? That would suck rocks. I've always been a fan of having a type that can do large-precision arithmetic, and `BigInteger` was a good start. I hope it makes it back into the final version of Orcas.
