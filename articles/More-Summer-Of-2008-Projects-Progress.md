# More Summer of 2008 Projects Progress

Saturday I went to pick up a gas tank for the grill. Haven't hooked it up yet, because the rest of the weekend was devoted to tearing up old felt and laying rock in front of the house in 5 different areas. I worked from 1 to 5 and then Liz realized that by the time I'd get done the sun would have set, so she called over a neighbor boy to help out. Good idea - everything was done by 7:30. It looks so much nicer than it did before. I'm also sore, sore, sore. My hands, my legs, my feet - ugh. But I guess when you're used to having your ass in a chair for 8 hours a day "real work" will do that to you :).

What's sad is that both of thos projects are still not "done". I have one area in the back yard that needs the re-rock treatment, and the grill still isn't operational. Hopefully by next weekend that will all be rectified.
