# "The Incredibles"

Liz and I saw this movie last night. I really enjoyed it. The action scenes were awesome, and the animation was amazing. Granted, the movie felt a little longer than it should have been, but it was worth the money (and the tailor character, Edna 'E' Mode, was hilarious). If you want to be a superhero, never get a cape with your suit.
