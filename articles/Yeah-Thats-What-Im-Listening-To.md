# Yeah, That's What I'm Listening To

For some reason yesterday, my brain remembered that great [1] 80s rock band Zebra. So I fired up my Zune to get some of their stuff and remember my high school days of long hair. Oddly enough, as I was started playing "No Tellin' Lies", the guy next to me looked at me and said, "Is that Zebra?" Wow - someone else actually remembers them!

[1] I'm being a bit sarcastic here - they weren't bad, but c'mon, how can you take a band seriously that has a song called "Bears"? And I know Rush has a song called "The Trees". Shut up.
