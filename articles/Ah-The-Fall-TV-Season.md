# Ah, the Fall TV Season

Last night Liz and I watches the season premieres of "Lost" and "South Park". "South Park" totally skewered WoW - "then everything will end in the world...of Warcraft", "I don't have an account - I have a life!". And "Lost" cleared up a lot of hanging plot lines. It's interesting to know that Jack was one of the Others all along, that Walt killed his father on the boat, that Sawyer and Kate are really brother and sister, that John could always walk (he just refused to after a nasty paper cut at the box factory he worked at ... something about being "scared"), and the island has a golf course that no one has found yet. Cool stuff.
