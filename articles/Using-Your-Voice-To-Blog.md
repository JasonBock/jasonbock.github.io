# Using Your Voice To Blog

[Scott blogs about](https://www.hanselman.com/blog/post/9d388967-5e76-49dc-b68c-cb71f463b9a5?guid=9d388967-5e76-49dc-b68c-cb71f463b9a5) using his voice instead of his hands to blog. Fortunately, I can type fairly fast, but, while I haven't had the pain Scott's talking about in my hands, sometimes my hands simply cannot keep up with my thoughts. I think this may be a good alternative to getting my ideas up on my web site.

As a side note, I used to keep a vocal diary when I was in grad school. I just popped in a tape, and started talking. It would be interesting to listen to those again, but that's literally **hours** of my ramblings, so I doubt I'll ever listen to those again :)
