# More Believer Music in the Future?

I just stumbled across an interview with Kurt Bachman, and this caught my attention:

> We have two more albums left for Metal Blade and I’m actually looking forward to the second one because I think the recording process will go much smoother. We know a little more about what we’re doing with the equipment that bought during Gabriel. Looking forward to it.

Oh sweet! At least 2 more albums from the Believer crew. I have fallen in love with "Gabriel" and I was hoping this wouldn't be final work from them.
