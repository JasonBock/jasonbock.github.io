# More NUnit Love and Passport 2.5

The project I'm on is the first where I'm really trying to use NUnit as much as I can. It's nice when you can change something, run your tests, and see if everything still works as expected. I'm sure I haven't caught all the bugs, and unit testing is not a cure-all, but it's still a good feeling.

By the way, if you're trying to use Passport, you may want to hold off on 2.5. I tried installing it on a couple of machines in the lab (one with XP Professional and the other is W2K Advanced Server) and both didn't like 2.5. I think it has something to do with .NET being on the machine as well, but I can't be sure. Reverting to 2.1 made the entire authorization process work as expected. Of course, the reason I'm using Passport in the first place is ... well, I have to.
