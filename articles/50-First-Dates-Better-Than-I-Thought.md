# "50 First Dates" - Better Than I Thought

Liz and I went to see "50 First Dates" this evening. I thought it would be a good comedy for us to see, but it was a lot better than that. Maybe my expectations were set lower to begin with, but the story had more substance to it than I anticipated. It's a good movie to see with the significant other.
