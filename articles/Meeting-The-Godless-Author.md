# Meeting the "Godless" Author

After work I went to a local bookstore to meet Pete Hautman, author of the book, "Godless". I haven't read the book yet, but I wanted to meet him because the main character has the same name as me, and the story sounded interesting. I asked Pete to autograph the book - what he did put a smile on my face:

![Godless](https://jasonbock.net/images/Godless.png "Godless")

"No, I'm Jason!" "No, **I'm** Jason!!!" I found that pretty funny.
