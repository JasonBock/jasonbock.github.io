# Golf Was ... Interesting Today

After cutting the grass and taking a SQL Server 2000 Transcender test (wow, I have some studying to do!), I decided to get a round in. The first 9 was good: 42. The second nine...wasn't as good. It was also getting a little scary at the end as it started getting really stormy out. Lots of lightning and darkening skies. That's never a good environment to be in when you're carrying 14 lightning rods in your golf bag. Needless to say, I got the hell out of Dodge and booked it home!
