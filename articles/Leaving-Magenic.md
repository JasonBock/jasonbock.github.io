# Leaving Magenic

Today is a day with mixed emotions. Last night I accepted an offer to join Intertech as an instructor/consultant. I'm really looking forward to working with them - lots of things are happening there that line up with what I'm looking to do. At the same time, it's never easy to leave a company, and I have enjoyed working at Magenic. All I can say is, onward and upward!

I got my copy of "A New Kind of Science" last night. Looks like an awesome book - I'm looking forward to reading it. I should also be getting my copy of "Vapor Trails" real-soon-now.
