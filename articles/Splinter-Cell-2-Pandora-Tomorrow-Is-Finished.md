# "Splinter Cell 2: Pandora Tomorrow" is Finished

I just finished SC2 tonight. Overall, I liked the game. It's pretty much like the 1st version - lots of sneaking around, knocking people out, or (when it's allowed) sniping the enemies. The ending took a while to finish, but the final animated ending was weak. It ended too abruptly. Furthermore, the storyline didn't seem that appropriate - I couldn't tell you why I had Sam Fisher do the things he did. But it was definitely worth the money. Now I need to go online with it and get my ass kicked.
