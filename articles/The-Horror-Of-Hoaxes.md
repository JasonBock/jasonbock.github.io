# The Horror of Hoaxes

This is why the new version of "The Amityville Horror" sucks. It's not because the acting may suck (which is quite possible); rather, the **entire story** is a hoax that was admitted by the Lutz family years ago. I don't mind a good horror movie now and then, but this story needs to die off and go away. It's all a bunch of crap.
