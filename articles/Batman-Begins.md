# "Batman Begins"

Liz and I rented this movie Friday night. I heard good things about this movie, and I wasn't disappointed. It was a very strong film - good acting all around and an interesting script. By far this was the best "Batman" movie ever made and I'd be interested in seeing if they make any sequels with these actors.
