# My Great Big Ball of Stress

Liz is staying overnight at the hospital tonight. Seems that her flu made her too dehydrated and that was causing labor-like symptoms. So they're pumping her full of fluids, and hopefully she can come home tomorrow. The upshot is that her parents are coming tomorrow to stay for a while to help out. We need it. The last month has been harder and harder to get things done around the house (and life in general). Plus I've had to take more unexpected PTO days in the last month and I'm basically pulling out of my days for next year as I'm running out (and I didn't want to do that, but I have no choice).

I know life could be 10 times worse, but I can't wait until this is all over and we have a healthy Ryan in our family ...
