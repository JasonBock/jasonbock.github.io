# "For the Blood is the Life"

I finished this book a while ago. It's a collection of "ghost" stories from Crawford. Sadly, I left it on a plane and I can't remember all of the story titles (but at least I finished it!). Overall the stories are fairly predictable but I found them fairly interesting.
