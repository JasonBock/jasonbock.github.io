# Genetic Vectorizing My High School Hair Picture

I ran [Roger's](https://rogerjohansson.blog) [new GV program](https://rogerjohansson.blog/2008/12/11/genetic-programming-mona-lisa-source-code-and-binaries/) on part of one of my classic hair pictures. Here's the results:

![Vectorizing Hair](https://jasonbock.net/images/Hair-Vectorize.png "Vectorizing Hair")

It's close.

What I thought would be kind of cool is to have a bunch of people process a frame of a movie up to a certain fitness value. Then combine all those images and play it back. I think it would look kind of cool. You'd need a lot of people to work on this, though, as the time to process one pictures takes a while. But having the "GV effect" applied to a scene of a movie? I think that would be a fun project to undertake!
