# Different Workout Today

I was planning on going to the gym today ... but then the company that dumped the river rock for us on Sunday came again today! I ran out of the house, yelling, "STOP!", but it was too late - they put another load in the driveway. They soon realized it was their fault, and they just called me back, saying, "you can have it for free."

Since I was thinking about getting a shed this summer, now I have even more rock to cover that up. So my workout today will consist of shoveling rock and moving it under our deck, instead of going to the gym. Hey, whatever makes me sweat :)
