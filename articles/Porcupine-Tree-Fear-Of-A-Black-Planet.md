# Porcupine Tree - "Fear of a Black Planet"

Another sign of my old age is that I'm getting really selective with my musical tastes. I knows what I likes, and damnit, I'm gonna listen to that! But being a musician, I also try to keep my ears open to different things. I just stumbled across a band called Porcupine Tree and I'm listening to their latest: "Fear of a Black Planet". It's definitely...different. It's rock with touches of a progressive, dissontant feel to it. Give it a whirl - I'm not sure if I'm completely sold on them, but it's definitely something that's a little out there and I'm groving on a couple of their songs.
