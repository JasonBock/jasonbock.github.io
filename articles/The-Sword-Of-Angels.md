# "The Sword of Angels"

I finished this book on Friday. This was the third of a trilogy - the other two being "The Eyes of God" and "The Devil's Armor". Overall, the series was somewhat interesting, but the writing was rather juvenille at times. It's hard to describe - the story is fairly vast, but I didn't feel like the characters were very deep either. It was passable enough for me to slog through all three books, but I have some reservations about reading Marco's work in the future. Pretty standard fantasy stuff - not bad, not good either.
