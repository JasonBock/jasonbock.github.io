# Mike ... More Recording!

Wednesday night Mike and I worked on the vocals for our songs. Well, last night I had the opportunity to stop by Mike's place again and help him out some more. We finally (!) got all of the vocal work done - that's a huge milestone that we've passed. Starting next week we'll be able to move into the mixing phase. There are a couple of musical/spoken-word things that I want to add, but that can wait until the mixing process.

I'm happy that the light is getting brighter. Part of it is that I'm really excited about what we've done, and part of it is ... I just want to move on! I've been working with Mike on these songs longer than Hayden has been alive, and it's time to call the finalizer on this material (sorry, bad .NET reference). Besides, we have to start touring sometime ... right, Mike?
