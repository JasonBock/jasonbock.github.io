# WTC Towers Collapse

I'm about to leave for vacation, but it's a sad day to start one. Seeing the WTC towers collapse as the result of a terrorist attack is pretty overwhelming - I can't imagine what it's like for those who were near it and/or are close to someone who was killed. This is a sad day for the world. For some reason, I think of the lyrics from one of Rush's songs: "Try to hold some faith in the goodness of humanity." That's hard to do today.
