# Deck Progress

Over the last month or so, a fair amount of progress has been made on our deck. Here's a couple of pictures to document the deck evolution:

![Deck Progress](https://jasonbock.net/images/Deck-Progress-1.png "Deck Progress")

The first thing I did is tear up all the sod and lay down some felt and put the edging in. I found out later that the guys had mismeasured a bit so the side where the post for the stairs goes was going to go right into the edging, so I eventually moved that over. By this time, they had drilled and poured the holes and put up the framing, except for the stair section.

![Deck Progress](https://jasonbock.net/images/Deck-Progress-2.png "Deck Progress")

Now all the framing is done and we got a load of river rock under the deck. Next week the guys should put the decking and rails up and we'll finally have a back door to our house!
