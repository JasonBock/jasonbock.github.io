# Has Anyone Modded the 360

I remember the good old days of the XBox. It didn't take long for someone to figure out how to mod it so people could ~~store tons of porn and illegal movies~~ use it as a cheap Linux box. But with the 360, I'm not hearing of anyone modding it. Not that I would want to do it to mine - I never modded my XBox - but I'm surprised that there's nothing out there yet. At least my initial web searches didn't find anything (and admittedly, this is probably something that won't be heavily publicized)...have you heard of a 360 being successfully cracked?
