# 20 Hours of Painting

I'm exhausted. My friend Andy and I spent the whole day painting the living room/dining room/foyer area. I was up from 6:30 AM to 3:00 AM the next day. It looks really nice (folky gold and some other rust-like color), but it was a lot of work. Cathedrial ceilings, weird corners ... you name it, we had to deal with it. But it's all done, and it's a dramatic difference. Once I get done with the traveling I'm going to paint the rest of rooms in the house, starting with the kitchen. The hardest rooms are now done - it's pretty much gravy from now on :).
