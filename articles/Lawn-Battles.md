# Lawn Battles

Ever since Liz and I bought our house 6 years ago (wow, has it been 6 years already?), we've used a lawn care service to do all sorts of lawn treatments for us. This year, we decided to "go it alone" and do all of the maintenance ourself. Yesterday I was looking at our lawn and...well, let's say I have a weed "issue". It's not bad, but if I didn't do something about it soon it would get really bad. I ran to Lowe's and got some weed killer stuff to spread on our lawn. I'll see how that works over the next week or so.

It's been nice having someone else do stuff for our house, but I realized that for the lawn we can handle it ourselves. At least that's what I'm trying to convince myself.
