# Review: "Why do Men Have Nipples?"

The subtitle of this book explains the content of this book better, as it contains questions that you'd be more inclinded to ask when you a bit tipsy. It's a quick read and it's somewhat entertaining, but it not heavy on substance. Leave it in the bathroom - I mean that as a compliment. It's a great book to read when you're spending a long time on the crapper - you may learn some things about your crap while you're reading.
