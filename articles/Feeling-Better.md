# Feeling Better

I'm glad I took the day off, as I'm feeling much better than I did this morning. As with most colds I get, I bet in the next 2-3 days I'll be dealing with a nose that runs faster than Lance Armstrong did in this years New York City Marathon, but I can manage that with little problem. Having a throat so raw I could barely talk is not good when I have to talk to a bunch of people on Saturday.
