# Late-Night Observations

This week I've been getting up around 2 or 3 AM to give Ryan a bottle. There's not much to do at that time, so I've been trying to record shows that are somewhat interesting and watch them when I feed him. However, I'm kind of out of it when I get up then, so I forget to watch the recorded shows and I channel-surf. I've hit the Sci-Fi channel in the process, and they've been playing some god-awful "Hellraiser" movies that are just plain ridiculous. I remember seeing the first one when I was younger and that kind of freaked me out a bit. I saw the second one and said, "that was stupid". After that, I decided not to waste my life watching the sequels. But ... lately I've had the misfortune of having "Hellraiser: Hellworld" on when I'm feeding Ryan. Dear lord, it's amazing the shit they'll record and play back for people!
